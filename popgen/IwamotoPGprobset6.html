A genetically heterogeneous population of kalo has a variance in the number of days to maturation of 40 days, whereas two inbred populations derived from it have a variance in the number of days to maturation of 10 days. What is the genetic variance (VG), the environmental variance (VE), and the broad-sense heritability (H2),for days to maturation in this population?
Vp <- 40
Ve <- 10
Vg <- Vp - Ve #given both populations of kalo are growing in the same environment, the difference in days to maturation should show the difference in genetic variance between a genetically heterogeneous pop and an inbred one. 


#h2 = Vg/Vp Broad sense heritability (H2) estimates the proportion of phenotypic variation that is due to total genetic variation
h2 <- Vg/Vp
print(h2)
## [1] 0.75

If the inbred lines were crossed, what would be the predicted variance in days to maturation of the F1 generation?

The predicted variance would be 10 because the genetic variance is 0, so Vp=Ve.
